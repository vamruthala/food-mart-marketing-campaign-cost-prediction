# Business Requirements Summary
- **Business question:** Estimate campaign cost and identify cost-efficient promotion, media, and location combinations.
- **Decision supported:** Compare modeled cost patterns when planning campaigns and budgets.
- **Scope:** Food Mart dataset, target `Cost`, 12 selected predictors.
- **Success measure:** Compare held-out prediction error; champion chosen by lowest validation ASE.
- **Open item:** Acquired-customer counts are not documented, so CAC per customer cannot be computed.
